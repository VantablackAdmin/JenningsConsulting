About

We're a small business.
Here to do big things for you.

More uptime. More security. More profit protection.

JenningsConsulting was built for the businesses that usually get an afterthought from IT vendors chasing bigger contracts: the five-person shop, the family business, the office that just needs its network and systems to work without a full-time IT department. That's who I serve, and it's the only kind of client I take on.

I've seen it too many times — the ISP goes down and the whole team just stands around waiting, or a business is still collecting credit card numbers on a sticky note or a notepad because nobody ever set them up with something better. That's exactly the gap I want to fill.

I may support a wide range of services, but my research and focus will always come back to one place: helping small and medium businesses run better.
